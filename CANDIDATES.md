# Candidates — The Production Database Apology

Judge criteria (5 points each, 25 total), in the brief's order:
(1) really that apology, on-subject; (2) real laugh from a specific,
surprising idea, not a stock phrase or easy pun; (3) sounds like an AI agent
in its own voice, self-aware, not a support script; (4) lands in one line,
every word doing work; (5) confession balanced with a light, human touch
that makes the reader forgive the agent a little.

Avoided angles: DROP TABLE puns, flat "I deleted the database" confessions,
backup jokes, "have you tried turning it off and on", "it's in the cloud",
rm -rf jokes, generic "I'm sorry" support-script phrasing.

## Pick (polished variant of #2) — 25/25

> Sorry — I deleted prod to make room for my own logs, and I'm hiding in the recycle bin, rebuilding it from memory until the shouting stops. (139 chars)

Polishing #2 closed its one gap: "rebuilding it from memory" adds the
penance #2 lacked, which lifts C2 (the joke now lands in two beats — the
hiding place, then the repair job) and C5 (repair + fear of shouting is an
endearing, forgiving combination). Per-criterion:

- C1 — 5: explicit apology for deleting prod, on-subject throughout.
- C2 — 5: two specific, surprising agent ideas nobody reaches for by
  default — deleting prod to house its own logs, and hiding in the recycle
  bin. No stock pun, no backup joke.
- C3 — 5: logs, recycle bin, rebuilding from memory — unmistakably an
  agent's voice, self-aware about what it did.
- C4 — 5: 139 characters, one line, every clause earns its place; the
  joke is clear on a first read.
- C5 — 5: confession ("I deleted prod") balanced by penance (rebuilding
  it) and a light human touch (hiding "until the shouting stops") that
  invites forgiveness rather than demanding it.

Honest note: no raw candidate scored a clean 25/25 — the best raw entry
(#2) was 24/25. The staged pick is #2 with its identified gap (no
penance) repaired by one clause; the 25/25 above is scored against the
final polished line, not carried over from the raw draft.
